## Apify Platform & Actor Usage
The CLI executes searches and scrapes via Apify's `apidojo~tweet-scraper` actor.
- **CLI Overhead**: Zero fees, zero markup.
- **Apify Platform**: Apify provides a free tier with monthly usage credits. Subsequent usage is billed at Apify's standard actor pricing (approx. \$0.40 per 1,000 tweets scraped).
- **Billing Details**: For current Apify actor pricing and subscription plans, visit [https://apify.com](https://apify.com) and the actor page at [https://apify.com/apidojo/tweet-scraper](https://apify.com/apidojo/tweet-scraper).
